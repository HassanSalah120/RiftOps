# Third-party notices

## backloop.dev

RiftOps can download a public loopback TLS certificate bundle from
[backloop.dev](https://github.com/perki/backloop.dev). The bundle lets Riot's
chat client establish a publicly trusted TLS connection to a proxy bound to the
user's own loopback interface. RiftOps does not copy the certificate or private
key into this repository; it validates and caches the current published bundle
at runtime.
